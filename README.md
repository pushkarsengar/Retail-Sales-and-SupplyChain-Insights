# Retail-Sales-and-SupplyChain-Insights
A professional 3-page Power BI dashboard for Global Sales, Profitability, and Logistics analysis.

# 📊 Global Sales & Operational Intelligence Dashboard

---

### **1. Project Title / Headline**
**Global Sales Analytics & Logistics Suite** *Example: 🚀 SalesGenius: End-to-End Business Performance Dashboard*

---

### **2. Short Description / Purpose**
A professional 3-page Power BI report designed to track global retail performance, analyze profit margins, and audit supply chain logistics.  
*Example: This tool bridges the gap between raw sales data and strategic decision-making for global retail managers.*

---

### **3. Tech Stack**
* **Power BI Desktop:** Dashboard authoring & visualization.
* **Power Query:** ETL, data cleaning, and transformation.
* **DAX:** Custom measures for Profit Margin %, YoY Growth, and Avg Shipping.
* **Data Modeling:** Star Schema implementation (Fact & Dimension tables).

---

### **4. Data Source**
Comprehensive Global Retail Dataset including Transactions, Geography, Product Categories, and Logistics details.

---

### **5. Features / Highlights**

* **Business Problem:** Fragmented global data makes it hard to identify "Profit-Bleeding" regions and high shipping costs.
* **Goal:** Provide a 360-degree view of Sales, Profitability, and Operations in one interactive tool.
* **Key Visuals:** - **Page 1:** Global Maps & KPI Cards for Revenue.
    - **Page 2:** Scatter Charts for Margin Analysis.
    - **Page 3:** Operational trends & Shipping cost breakdown.
* **Business Impact:** Identified that 18.09% profit margin is driven primarily by the Consumer segment, while certain regions require logistics optimization.
